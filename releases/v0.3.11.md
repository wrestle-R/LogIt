- Set your own minimum for Sleep. Short nights stay in your history, and a night that reaches the goal logs the activity once.
- The Sleep page puts your latest night and goal up top. Open Tracking from the main menu to find Sleep, Health Connect, app time rules, and screen time limits.
- The Sleep widget shows duration at its smallest size, adds bedtime and wake time when there is room, and shows the last seven nights in larger layouts.
- Phone sleep estimates have their own setting, separate from app time and screen time tracking.
